How to use this

1. Set the number of tension lugs on your drum (count the rods around the rim), and pick which head you are on — batter or reso. Each head keeps its own set of readings.

2. Hold your phone 6–12″ above the head. Tap firmly with a stick about 1″ in from the rim, next to lug 1. The app grabs the pitch and moves to the next lug automatically. You can also tap a lug on the diagram to re-measure it.

3. Resting one finger lightly in the center of the head while you tap makes the lug pitch cleaner to read.

A drum is “in tune with itself” when every lug rings at the same pitch. Green = matches the others, red = off. Higher than the rest → loosen that rod slightly; lower → tighten. Quarter-turns at a time.

Wait about a second between taps — the app analyses a short window of audio after each hit, and crowding them makes readings bleed together.

Your readings are saved on this device, so you can close the page and pick up where you left off.

Rough size guide (lug-tap pitch, medium tuning): kick under 72 Hz · 16″ floor 72–105 · 14″ floor 105–130 · 13″ tom 130–160 · 12″ tom 160–195 · 10″ tom 195–235 · snare 235+. Tuning changes everything, so a slack 12″ can read like a 13″ — treat the guess as a hint, not a fact.

Microphone access

DrumTuner listens through your phone's mic to read the pitch of each tap. Audio is analyzed live on your device only — nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. Your browser will ask to confirm.
